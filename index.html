About

I am a Research and Insight Analyst with experience turning complex quantitative and qualitative evidence into clear, practical recommendations. My work has covered healthcare, technology, energy, customer and commercial research. I have designed surveys, conducted stakeholder and expert interviews, analysed large and varied datasets, built market forecasts and scenario models, and translated findings into reports, dashboards, whitepapers and presentations for senior decision makers. I am particularly experienced in bringing together information from different sources, checking data quality, investigating conflicting evidence and understanding the wider factors behind the numbers. My projects have included customer and patient research, market and competitor analysis, demand forecasting, government and policy research, technology trends and strategic decision support. I work primarily with Excel, Power BI, SQL and research tools, alongside quantitative and qualitative research methods. I enjoy roles where the question is not immediately clear and the job is to investigate the evidence, understand what it really means and communicate the answer in a way that people can act on.
